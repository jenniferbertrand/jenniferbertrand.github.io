About

I am a postdoctoral researcher studying how human perception, experience, and intention are expressed through digital interaction and movement. My work combines human–computer interaction, multimodal sensing, and digital health technologies for scalable, real-world applications.

Research Areas

  • Digital measurement of human behavior: Quantifying movement, perception, and intention through interaction.
  • Human–computer interaction: Studying how visual, haptic, and interactive feedback shape user behavior and experience.
  • Multimodal sensing: Eye tracking, cursor and mouse tracking, and markerless motion capture.
  • Remote and scalable assessment: Designing digital tools for at-home and real-world measurement of cognition and physical function.
  • Digital health & UX: Translating interaction data into clinically and user-meaningful insights.
